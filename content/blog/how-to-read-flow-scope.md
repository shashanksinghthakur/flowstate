Flow scope is one instrument with four lenses. Here's how to read it.

## The dial picks the lens

Turn it, drag it, scroll on it, or tap a label. **Chains**, **Platforms**, **Assets** and **Regions** each answer a different question about the same market.

## Left is where money came from, right is where it went

The bright bar in the middle is whatever you're looking at. Bands on the left feed into it. Bands on the right flow out of it. A band's thickness is its share of that side. The moving dots go faster on bigger flows.

## Solid is measured, hatched is estimated

Solid bands come from balances that were observed directly. Hatched bands are inferred, for example a platform's TVL change, which also moves with token prices.

## Tap to go one layer deeper

Every band with a name can be traced. Try this path: Platforms, then Hyperliquid Bridge, then Hyperliquid perps, then BTC. The path you took stays at the top, so you can step back at any point.

## The readout tells you if it matters

The big figure is the net flow. The gauge compares it to the size of what you're looking at: $300M is huge for a small chain and noise for Ethereum. The sentence underneath says what happened in plain words.

## Use the command bar

Press `/` and type a chain, platform or market, then hit GO. Flow scope jumps straight to it.
