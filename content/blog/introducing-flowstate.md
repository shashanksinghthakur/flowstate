Prices are the last thing to move. By the time a chart breaks out, the money behind it has already crossed a bridge, landed on a venue and picked a market.

Flowstate is built to show that path.

## What it tracks

- **Chains:** net stablecoin dollars arriving at and leaving every chain, plus the mints and redemptions that bring dollars in or out of crypto.
- **Platforms:** TVL moving between DeFi protocols, venues and bridges.
- **Assets:** market-cap rotation between narratives like AI, RWA, memecoins and DeFi.
- **Regions:** buy and sell pressure during Asia, Europe and Americas trading hours, plus the Coinbase premium as a read on US demand.
- **TradFi:** ETF money flow, Treasury yields, the dollar and US liquidity.

## Trace, don't just watch

Every band in Flow scope can be tapped. Tap a chain and you see which platforms took its dollars. Tap the Hyperliquid bridge and you see margin arriving, then which markets it traded into.

## Honest about method

Some flows are measured directly, like stablecoin supply on a chain. Others are estimated, like TVL changes that include price moves. Flowstate marks every band as one or the other, so you always know what you're reading.
